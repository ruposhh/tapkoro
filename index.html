Questions and answers

What is NFC?

NFC lets your phone read a tag when you hold it close. Tap your phone on a TapKoro stand or tag, and it connects to the WiFi or opens the page saved on it.

Which phones work?

Your phone needs NFC, and NFC must be switched on. The WiFi Connect Tag works on Android only, not on iOS. For the other products, message us on WhatsApp and we will tell you which phones work.

Does it need a battery or charging?

No. TapKoro stands and tags need no battery and no charging.

Is it waterproof?

The Instagram NFC Car Tag is waterproof, and the NFC Cat Collar Tag is water-resistant and durable. For other products, please ask us on WhatsApp.

Can I choose Facebook or Instagram?

Yes. The Follow Stand comes as a Facebook stand or an Instagram stand. You choose on the product page.

How do I get my own link or WiFi on the tag?

When we confirm your order on WhatsApp, send us the link or the WiFi details you want on your tag.

What does the Cat Collar Tag do?

It is made to share your contact and location with the person who taps it, so a lost cat can find the way home.
