Privacy

At checkout you type your name, mobile number, address and an optional note. This website uses them only to write your order message. It does not save them on a server.

Your details reach us only when you press Send in WhatsApp. WhatsApp handles messages under its own terms.

Your cart is saved in your own browser so it stays when you refresh the page. You can clear it any time by removing the items.

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google may see your internet address. Our hosting provider may also keep normal server logs. This website has no ads or tracking tools.
